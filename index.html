📖 Ludo Rules

• Winning Goal: Navigate all 4 pins into the center victory triangle to win the game!


• Unlock: Roll a 6 to exit the base onto your colored starting track.


• Movement: Click any highlighted pin to advance clockwise along the 52-step path.


• Safe Zones (☆): Star tiles protect your pins from being captured.


• Capturing: Land on an opponent's pin to send it back to base and get a bonus roll!


• Three 6s Rule: Rolling 3 consecutive 6s forfeits the 3rd roll and immediately ends your turn!


• Turn Timer & 3 Chances Rule: Each player has 10 seconds per turn. If a player fails to play 3 times, they are eliminated (Disqualified)!
